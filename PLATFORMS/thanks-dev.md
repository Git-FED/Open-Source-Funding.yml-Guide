# thanks.dev

> A B2B dependency-funding model that distributes company support across open-source dependency trees.

## What it is

This is not mainly a public tip jar. It is a corporate allocation mechanism.

## Payment rails

Payout rails and eligibility are model-specific; verify current maintainer onboarding.

## Fee lens

Do not compare it directly with a one-time tip fee without understanding the distribution model. Fees are not static. Confirm the official pricing page before publishing a recommendation.

## Strengths

- Clear audience fit when the project matches the platform model.
- A recognizable support path can reduce donor hesitation.
- Can work well as one half of a two-rail setup.

## Trade-offs

- Availability, identity checks, payout timing, and chargeback policy vary by country.
- A platform fee is only one line in the total cost; processor, FX, payout, and tax costs may sit elsewhere.
- Do not promise a tax deduction, legal status, or payout eligibility without checking the current official rules.

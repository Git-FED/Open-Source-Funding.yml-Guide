# GitHub Sponsors

> Native funding inside GitHub. Best first stop for an individual maintainer whose audience already lives in GitHub.

## What it is

GitHub Sponsors is a funding platform, not the card network. Payout onboarding and card processing are handled through connected payment infrastructure.

## Payment rails

Card via Stripe Connect; invoice options may exist for organizations.

## Fee lens

Personal accounts are often presented as 0% platform fee; organization economics can differ. Verify current terms. Fees are not static. Confirm the official pricing page before publishing a recommendation.

## Strengths

- Clear audience fit when the project matches the platform model.
- A recognizable support path can reduce donor hesitation.
- Can work well as one half of a two-rail setup.

## Trade-offs

- Availability, identity checks, payout timing, and chargeback policy vary by country.
- A platform fee is only one line in the total cost; processor, FX, payout, and tax costs may sit elsewhere.
- Do not promise a tax deduction, legal status, or payout eligibility without checking the current official rules.

## Practical profile

- **Difficulty:** See [Difficulty & Age](../GUIDES/platform-difficulty-and-age.md).
- **Country check:** Start with [Country Relevance](../GUIDES/country-relevance.md).

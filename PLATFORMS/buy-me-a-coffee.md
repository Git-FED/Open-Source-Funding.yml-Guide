# Buy Me a Coffee

> A fast, mobile-friendly tip page for maintainers and creators.

## What it is

It is a funding storefront; the exact payout and processing experience depends on its current payment integration.

## Payment rails

Card-oriented flow; verify payout country and processor terms.

## Fee lens

The brief uses a typical 5% platform fee plus processor costs as an orientation point. Fees are not static. Confirm the official pricing page before publishing a recommendation.

## Strengths

- Clear audience fit when the project matches the platform model.
- A recognizable support path can reduce donor hesitation.
- Can work well as one half of a two-rail setup.

## Trade-offs

- Availability, identity checks, payout timing, and chargeback policy vary by country.
- A platform fee is only one line in the total cost; processor, FX, payout, and tax costs may sit elsewhere.
- Do not promise a tax deduction, legal status, or payout eligibility without checking the current official rules.

## Practical profile

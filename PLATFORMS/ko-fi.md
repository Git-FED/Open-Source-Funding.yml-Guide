# Ko-fi

> A lightweight tip, membership, shop, and commission page that can connect directly to Stripe and/or PayPal.

## What it is

Ko-fi generally acts as the storefront while your connected payment accounts handle the money movement.

## Payment rails

Stripe and PayPal, depending on what you connect and what your country supports.

## Fee lens

One-time tips and memberships/shop actions can have different platform treatment. Fees are not static. Confirm the official pricing page before publishing a recommendation.

## Strengths

- Clear audience fit when the project matches the platform model.
- A recognizable support path can reduce donor hesitation.
- Can work well as one half of a two-rail setup.

## Trade-offs

- Availability, identity checks, payout timing, and chargeback policy vary by country.
- A platform fee is only one line in the total cost; processor, FX, payout, and tax costs may sit elsewhere.
- Do not promise a tax deduction, legal status, or payout eligibility without checking the current official rules.

# IssueHunt

> A bounty platform where money is attached to a specific issue or task.

## What it is

IssueHunt is a funding platform for outcomes, not general recurring support.

## Payment rails

Fiat and crypto partner rails can vary.

## Fee lens

The brief uses a 20% bounty fee as an orientation point. Fees are not static. Confirm the official pricing page before publishing a recommendation.

## Strengths

- Clear audience fit when the project matches the platform model.
- A recognizable support path can reduce donor hesitation.
- Can work well as one half of a two-rail setup.

## Trade-offs

- Availability, identity checks, payout timing, and chargeback policy vary by country.
- A platform fee is only one line in the total cost; processor, FX, payout, and tax costs may sit elsewhere.
- Do not promise a tax deduction, legal status, or payout eligibility without checking the current official rules.

# Open Collective

> A transparent budgeting and fiscal-hosting model for teams and communities.

## What it is

The platform and the fiscal host are distinct. The host may receive funds, handle compliance, and pay approved expenses.

## Payment rails

Cards, PayPal, transfers, and other rails depend on the selected host.

## Fee lens

Host and processor charges can make the all-in range materially higher than a tip jar. Fees are not static. Confirm the official pricing page before publishing a recommendation.

## Strengths

- Clear audience fit when the project matches the platform model.
- A recognizable support path can reduce donor hesitation.
- Can work well as one half of a two-rail setup.

## Trade-offs

- Availability, identity checks, payout timing, and chargeback policy vary by country.
- A platform fee is only one line in the total cost; processor, FX, payout, and tax costs may sit elsewhere.
- Do not promise a tax deduction, legal status, or payout eligibility without checking the current official rules.
